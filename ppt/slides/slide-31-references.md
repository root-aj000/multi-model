# Slide 31 — References

## What to Say (Speaker Notes)
"Here are the **key references** that made this project possible. The **BERT paper** (Devlin et al., 2018) introduced the transformer-based language model that DistilBERT is based on. The **DistilBERT paper** (Sanh et al., 2019) showed how to compress BERT while retaining 97% of its performance. The **ResNet paper** (He et al., 2016) introduced residual connections that enabled very deep CNNs. The **CLIP paper** (Radford et al., 2021) from OpenAI showed how to train a model on image-text pairs at scale. I also used the **EasyOCR** and **PaddleOCR** documentation, the **PyTorch** documentation, the **HuggingFace Transformers** library, the **FastAPI** documentation, and the **Next.js** documentation. All of these are open source and freely available."
