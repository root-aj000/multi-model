# Slide 4 — Motivation & Real-World Use Cases

## What to Say (Speaker Notes)
"Why does this problem matter? Because **advertising is a huge industry**, and almost every step of it can benefit from automatic ad understanding. Let me give you **6 real-world examples**. First, **programmatic ad buying** — when an ad is uploaded, it must be tagged before it enters the real-time auction. Second, **marketing analytics** — companies want to track sentiment and emotion trends across campaigns. Third, **brand safety** — we want to automatically flag unsafe or questionable ads. Fourth, **audience targeting** — we can predict the age group an ad is meant for. Fifth, **CTR prediction** — before launching, we can score how likely an ad is to be clicked. Sixth, **A/B testing** — pick the strongest creative variant automatically. So this one model has **many practical uses**."

## Visual Suggestion
- Use **6 small icons** (one per use case) — a gavel for ad buying, a chart for analytics, a shield for safety, etc.
- Arrange them in a **2×3 grid** with short captions.
- Color-code by industry: marketing (blue), safety (red), analytics (green).

## Key Talking Points
- This slide answers the **"so what?"** question — why should anyone care?
- Mention that **all 6 use cases** are served by the **same single model** — that's the power of multi-task learning.
