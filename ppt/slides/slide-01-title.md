# Slide 1 — Title Slide

## What to Say (Speaker Notes)
"Hello everyone! Today I'm going to present my project called **Multi-Model Prediction System for Ad-Creative Classification**. In simple words, this is a smart computer program that can look at an advertisement — both the picture and the words inside it — and automatically figure out 9 different things about it, like what mood it has, who it is meant for, and whether it is safe to show. The technical name of the model is **FG_MFN**, which stands for **Fine-Grained Multi-Modal Fusion Network**. I'll explain every part step by step so that even if you have never done machine learning before, you will be able to follow along."

## Visual Suggestion
- Use a **dark navy background** with **white and teal text**.
- Add a small **logo or icon** in the top-right corner (a brain + eye icon works well).
- Keep the title **large** (around 40–44 pt) and the rest smaller (around 18–22 pt).
- Optional: a faint background image of an ad creative collage, blurred.

## Key Talking Points
- This is the **first slide** — keep it clean and professional.
- Smile, introduce yourself, and state the project name clearly.
- Mention that the talk will be about **20 minutes** with **5 minutes for questions** at the end.
